        ABOUT SCHEMEMATCH

        Making scheme information easier to understand.

        SchemeMatch is a government scheme discovery prototype created to help people explore potentially relevant opportunities and understand eligibility requirements in simpler language.

        Our approach focuses on transparent requirement checks, identifying missing information, and directing users to verified official sources.
